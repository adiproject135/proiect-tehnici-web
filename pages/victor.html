VICTOR / DESK AREA CONNECT THE DETAILS
PERSONALITY

Always sharing.

Victor often talks about astronomy and shares his observations. Over time, those details can reveal his routines and interests.

CLUE VICTOR / SECURITY HABIT

The details add up

His reading list and comments point to the subjects he follows. Someone could use that information to make a phishing message feel personal.

“You should see Orion tonight.”
SECURITY TAKEAWAY

Personal details can be used against you

Public hobbies and routines can help attackers build believable stories. They should not be used in passwords or security answers.
